How to Distinguish a Reaction

Allergic reactions can look different every time. It is vital to recognize these common signs immediately:

🤧 Sneezing / Runny Nose
👀 Itchy / Watery Eyes
🩹 Itchy Skin / Rashes
👄 Swelling of Lips/Tongue
🫁 Trouble Breathing
🤢 Digestive Issues

Important: Symptoms vary by exposure—ranging from respiratory issues with pollen to severe airway swelling (anaphylaxis) with food. If severe symptoms occur: Act fast and use Epi!
