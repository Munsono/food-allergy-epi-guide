How to Identify a Reaction

Allergic reactions can look different every time. Common signs include: sneezing, runny or stuffy nose, itchy/watery eyes, and itchy skin or rashes. Symptoms vary by exposure type—ranging from respiratory issues with pollen to digestive issues or severe airway swelling (anaphylaxis) with food/drugs. Act fast and use epi!
